These three PNG files are exact pixel crops of the Attribute row in the
published appendix montage. They have not been generated, retouched,
recolored, or resized for this website. The upstream montage was built
from retained frozen PASS simulation renders at 2x scale.

The question, A/B candidates, expected preferences, and saved Molmo-7B-D
outputs are linked to the same attr_000000 item IDs in the frozen manifest
and public judgment file. This is one illustrative case, not an aggregate
performance estimate.
