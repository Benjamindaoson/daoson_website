我长期追问的几个问题 Research Agenda

问题一 question.one

后训练如何带来可验证的能力增益？ How can post-training produce verifiable capability gains?

关注奖励建模、评估设计、捷径审计与检查点分析，而不是只报告单一分数。 I focus on reward modeling, evaluation design, shortcut auditing, and checkpoint analysis rather than a single score.

问题二 question.two

多模态模型是否理解了它声称理解的约束？ Do multimodal models understand the constraints they claim to understand?

用可测量的物理或任务约束区分真实理解、视觉关联和数据集捷径。 Measurable physical or task constraints separate genuine understanding from visual correlation and dataset shortcuts.

问题三 question.three

哪些 Agent 系统值得被认真构建？ Which agent systems deserve serious engineering?

只关注场景足够具体、工具与状态边界清楚、能够被验证和恢复的系统。 Only systems with a concrete use case, explicit tool and state boundaries, and verifiable recovery are worth building.
