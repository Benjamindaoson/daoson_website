关于我的工作HOW I WORK

把问题做具体，
把结果讲清楚。Make the problem concrete.
Make the evidence clear.

从制造业数字化，到企业智能体，再到模型后训练，我习惯把业务问题拆成可以执行、验证和持续改进的系统。既参与需求与方案设计，也负责核心研发与交付；研究中则用受控实验追问：能力提升来自哪里，什么时候会失效？From manufacturing systems to enterprise agents and model post-training, I turn business problems into systems that can be executed, tested, and improved. I work across customer discovery, architecture, core development, and delivery. In research, controlled experiments help me ask where gains come from and when they break down.
