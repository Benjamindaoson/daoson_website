关于我的工作HOW I WORK

把问题做具体，
把结果讲清楚。Make the problem concrete.
Make the evidence clear.

数据分析中，先约束业务语义，再让 Agent 调用工具；模型评估中，改变一个条件，观察答案是否合理变化；机器人研究中，先核查数据来源，再判断比较能说明什么。这些具体取舍，构成了我的工程与研究记录。In data analysis, constrain business semantics before an agent calls tools. In evaluation, change one condition and check the response. In robot research, inspect data provenance before interpreting a comparison. These decisions connect the engineering and research documented here.
