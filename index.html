Research × Engineering × Industry

大模型 → 多模态智能 → 决策智能 LLMs → Multimodal Intelligence → Decision Intelligence

我的路线不是堆模型或 Agent：从学习偏好与判断，到理解视觉与物理证据，再到把理解转化为可验证的决策与行动。每一层都用研究问题、工程系统和真实产业场景来验证。 My path is not a collection of models or agents: from learning preferences and judgment, to grounding in visual and physical evidence, to turning understanding into verifiable decisions and actions. Each layer is tested through research, engineering, and industry problems.
