RESEARCH AGENDA

我持续追问的三个问题Three questions I keep returning to

01

Reliable Agent Systems

长程 Agent 如何管理状态、工具、失败恢复与人工升级，使一次成功变成可重复、可审计的执行。How long-horizon agents manage state, tools, recovery, and human escalation so one successful run becomes repeatable and auditable execution.

02

Evidence-dependent Evaluation

当模型给出正确答案时，怎样判断它是否真正依赖了应该使用的视觉或任务证据，而不是捷径。When a model is correct, how can we tell whether it actually relied on the intended visual or task evidence rather than a shortcut?

03

Grounded Multimodal Intelligence

如何用测量、物理约束和反事实干预，让多模态模型的判断从相关性走向可验证 grounding。How measurement, physical constraints, and counterfactual interventions can move multimodal reasoning from correlation toward verifiable grounding.
