关于我的工作HOW I WORK

把问题做具体，
把结果讲清楚。Make the problem concrete.
Make the evidence clear.

我关心系统如何完成真实任务，也关心模型在什么条件下会失效。从 Agent 的调查过程，到模型面对变化时的表现，我习惯把设计、验证与局限一起记录下来。I care about how systems complete real tasks and when models fail. From an agent’s investigation to a model’s response to change, I document the design, the evidence, and the limits together.
