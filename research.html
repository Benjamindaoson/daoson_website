INDEPENDENT RESEARCH / PHYSICAL AI

世界模型何时真正改善机器人决策？When do world models improve robot decisions?

我研究动作条件世界模型的预测能力，何时能够转化为更可靠的机器人闭环决策。重点关注反事实动作比较、长时域误差，以及预测准确率与实际决策价值之间的差距。I investigate when action-conditioned world-model predictions translate into more reliable closed-loop robot decisions, focusing on counterfactual action comparisons, long-horizon errors, and the gap between predictive accuracy and decision value.

CORE RESEARCH QUESTION

预测得更准，
是否就能做出更好的决策？Does better prediction
lead to better decisions?

一个世界模型可以生成看似合理的未来，却仍然错误地比较候选动作。我的研究重点不是单独追求视频或状态预测指标，而是检验模型能否保留与决策相关的反事实差异，并在分布变化和长时域规划中维持可靠性。A world model can generate plausible futures yet still compare candidate actions incorrectly. Rather than optimizing video or state-prediction metrics in isolation, I study whether a model preserves decision-relevant counterfactual differences and remains reliable under distribution shift and long-horizon planning.

这是正在推进的研究议程；各项目页面会区分已验证结果、负面结果和待完成的实验。This is an ongoing research agenda. Individual project records distinguish validated results, negative findings, and experiments that remain to be done.

反事实可靠性Counterfactual reliability

相同初态下，不同动作的预测差异是否可信？模型是否识别了真正由动作引起的变化？Are predicted differences between actions trustworthy from the same initial state? Does the model capture changes actually caused by the action?

02 / LONG HORIZON

长时域决策可靠性Long-horizon decision reliability

预测误差如何累积并改变动作排序？不确定性是否能指导规划时域、重新观测与恢复？How do prediction errors accumulate and alter action rankings? Can uncertainty guide planning horizon, re-observation, and recovery?

01 / WORLD MODELSEARLY-STAGE RESEARCH

WorldDynamics ↗

Action-conditioned & counterfactual world models

研究从同一物理状态出发的不同动作分支，以及干预不足时如何表达不确定性。公开仓库包含可审计的简化基线、验证测试和后续物理实验设计；目前尚未证明配对分支监督优于独立监督。Studies alternative action futures from the same physical state and uncertainty under sparse interventions. The public repository includes auditable simplified baselines, tests, and a plan for physical validation; it has not established that paired-branch supervision outperforms independent supervision.

02 / ROBOT LEARNINGSIMULATION STUDY

WorldPolicy ↗

Robot policy learning & model-guided control

在 MuJoCo FetchPush 仿真中比较状态输入的 ACT、Diffusion Policy、学习动力学规划与物理 Oracle。公开结果包含不同种子和配对初态评估；不是官方 LIBERO、语言条件 VLA 或真实机器人实验。Compares state-conditioned ACT, Diffusion Policy, learned-dynamics planning, and a physics oracle in MuJoCo FetchPush simulation. The public results include seeded and paired-initial-state evaluations; they are not official LIBERO, language-conditioned VLA, or real-robot experiments.

Training, recovery & inference infrastructure

RESEARCH PRACTICE

证据先于结论。Evidence before claims.

我重视相同初态干预、匹配预算的对照、独立测试、多随机种子和失败案例分析，也会区分仿真与真实机器人结果、预测指标与任务成功率。公开记录明确标注已完成、验证中和计划中的工作。I emphasize same-state interventions, budget-matched baselines, independent tests, multiple seeds, and failure analysis. I distinguish simulation from real-robot results, predictive metrics from task success, and completed work from ongoing or planned experiments.

目前公开的项目仓库是研究代码与实验记录，不代表已经发表或经过同行评审的论文。The public repositories contain research code and experimental records; they are not presented as published or peer-reviewed papers.
