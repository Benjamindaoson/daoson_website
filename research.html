RESEARCH / WORLD MODELS · ROBOT LEARNING · MULTIMODAL AI

世界模型何时真正有助于决策？When do world models improve decisions?

我研究动作条件世界模型的预测能力，何时能够转化为机器人更可靠的闭环决策。关注长时域误差、反事实动作比较，以及预测准确率与实际任务成功率之间的差距。I investigate when action-conditioned prediction translates into more reliable closed-loop robot decisions, with a focus on long-horizon errors, counterfactual action comparisons, and the gap between predictive accuracy and task success.

RESEARCH AGENDA

核心研究问题Research questions

一个世界模型可以生成看似合理的未来，却仍然选错动作。我希望用受控干预和可复现的闭环评估，研究哪些预测能力真正服务于决策。A world model can produce plausible futures and still rank actions incorrectly. I use controlled interventions and reproducible closed-loop evaluations to study which predictive capabilities actually matter for decisions.

  • 反事实可靠性：相同初态下，不同动作的预测差异是否可信？Counterfactual reliability: Can predicted differences between actions be trusted from the same initial state?
  • 长时域决策：预测误差如何影响动作排序和闭环规划？Long-horizon decisions: How do predictive errors affect action ranking and closed-loop planning?
  • 计算与数据取舍：什么条件下，训练世界模型比直接训练策略更值得？Compute and data tradeoffs: When does learning a world model justify its cost relative to direct policy learning?

相邻兴趣包括 VLA、具身智能、机器人学习，以及多模态基础模型。这些是研究方向，不代表所有方向都已有成熟成果。Related interests include VLA, embodied intelligence, robot learning, and multimodal foundation models. These are research interests, not claims of completed results in every area.

SELECTED RESEARCH / PUBLIC CODE

公开研究记录Research projects and evidence

WorldDynamics ↗

Action-conditioned and counterfactual world models

研究从同一物理状态出发的不同动作分支，以及干预不足时如何表达不确定性。公开仓库当前包含可审计的简化基线、验证测试和后续物理实验设计；尚未证明配对分支监督优于独立监督。Investigates alternative action futures from the same physical state and uncertainty under sparse interventions. The public repository currently includes auditable simplified baselines, tests, and a plan for physical validation; it has not established an advantage for paired-branch supervision.

WorldPolicy ↗

Robot policy learning and world-model-guided control

在 MuJoCo FetchPush 仿真中比较状态输入的 ACT、Diffusion Policy、学习动力学规划与物理 Oracle。公开结果包含不同种子和配对初态的评估；当前不能当作官方 LIBERO、语言条件 VLA 或真实机器人实验。Compares state-conditioned ACT, Diffusion Policy, learned-dynamics planning, and a physics oracle in MuJoCo FetchPush simulation. Public results report seeded and paired-initial-state evaluations; they are not official LIBERO, language-conditioned VLA, or real-robot results.

WorldSystems ↗

World-model training and inference infrastructure

探索受限 GPU 条件下世界模型训练、断点恢复、CUDA Graph 推理和部署接口。公开证据覆盖部分可重复的训练与系统测试；这仍是进行中的研究工程工作，而非完整的生产级平台。Explores world-model training, checkpoint recovery, CUDA Graph inference, and serving interfaces under constrained GPU resources. Public evidence covers selected reproducible training and systems tests; this remains research engineering in progress, not a completed production platform.

RESEARCH PRACTICE

可复核的研究，而不只是演示Evidence before claims

我重视同初态干预、匹配预算的对照、独立测试、多随机种子、失败案例，以及预测质量和真实决策价值之间的区别。项目成果会按照公开证据区分已实现、仍在验证和计划中的工作；未经过同行评审的仓库不标为已发表论文。My evaluations emphasize same-state interventions, budget-matched baselines, independent tests, multiple seeds, failure analysis, and the distinction between prediction quality and decision value. Public records distinguish implemented, under-validation, and planned work; code repositories are not labeled as peer-reviewed publications.
